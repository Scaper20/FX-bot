# Long daily history (for weekly/monthly trend following)
- `*_fred_daily.csv`: FRED (Federal Reserve H.10 noon buying rates in New York), daily close, from 1971 (EUR/USD from 1999). Series ids: DEXUSUK, DEXUSEU, DEXJPUS, DEXSZUS, DEXCAUS, DEXUSAL, DEXUSNZ, DEXSDUS, DEXNOUS. Downloaded 20 Nov 2026.
- `XAUUSD_futures_daily.csv`, `XAGUSD_futures_daily.csv`: COMEX gold / silver continuous front-month futures (Yahoo GC=F, SI=F), daily close from Aug 2000. Futures, not spot: small jumps at contract rolls. Yahoo data is for personal research; don't redistribute.
