1 · CRT (Candle Range Theory)

{{ strategies.crt.evidence }}

What counts as a setup (CRT)

  1. Range candle: a finished D1 or H4 candle on Exness server time (GMT+0). Its high, low and 50% level are the range.
  2. Sweep: the next candle (or the one after, if the middle candle stayed inside) trades beyond one side (past the range candle's high or low) and closes back inside the range candle's body, between its open and close; a close that is only inside the wick doesn't count. Sweeping both sides doesn't count either.
  3. Trigger on a lower timeframe (D1 → H1 or M15; H4 → M15 or M5): a CISD (close beyond the open of the run of candles that made the sweep) or an MSS (close beyond the last swing before the sweep). The setup is armed until then, for up to two range candles. If price goes past the sweep extreme first, it is cancelled.

Your CRT rules: what gets recommended

  • Trigger in the London (07:00–16:00 London) or New York (08:00–17:00 New York) session, not Friday after 15:00 New York.
  • With the daily direction (last daily close beyond a swing high = up, beyond a swing low = down), or against it only when the sweep ran into an older higher-timeframe order block or FVG on the left.
  • At least 1 : 3 reward to risk after spread.

Setups that fail a rule are still listed, collapsed, so you can see what the rules removed.

Entry options

  • Entries: market at the trigger close; limit at the lower-timeframe FVG (edge or middle); limit at the order block (open or middle). Order blocks count only if they took liquidity and left an FVG.
  • Stops, sized for the spread: beyond the sweep extreme plus 2 spreads (default), or plus 0.1 ATR; the tight FVG/OB stop is shown only when the spread is under 15% of the risk.
  • Targets: the opposite side of the range or the next swing beyond it, all at once or half off at the 50% level with the stop moved to entry.
  • Order rules: an unfilled limit is dropped when the second range candle after the sweep candle closes (D1 about 2 days, H4 about 8 hours), or earlier if price reaches the 50% level first. Tested (v13): fills after price already reached 50% lose about −0.4R each, so the cancel stays.
  • Ranking: by each option's 2021–2024 average result, shrunk towards the overall average for small samples. Sample size is always shown.

Shown as checklist, not as filters

Killzone, FVG and order block present, both CISD and MSS, mid-timeframe MSS, HTF MSS, MSnR level, sweep candle size and depth. None of them changed results versus chance in any test from 2021 to 2026, so they are information for your judgment, not rules.

2 · Order block (H1 order block · D1+H4 bias)

What counts as a setup (order block)

A full strategy since 25 Nov 2026 (your decision). Every setup that meets all four rules is recommended, with its own results on Performance. Evidence, honestly: {{ strategies.smc_ob.evidence }} So treat its live record, not the backtest, as the real test.

  1. Bias: D1 and H4 market structure both point the same way (direction of the last close beyond a confirmed swing high or low).
  2. Market structure shift: on H1, a candle closes beyond the latest swing low (sells) or swing high (buys) in that direction.
  3. Entry: limit order at the open of the order block (the last opposite candle at the extreme of the move that took liquidity and left an FVG). Valid for 12 H1 candles.
  4. Stop: beyond the extreme of the move plus 2 spreads. Target: 3R (before the spread, exactly as tested, so after spread a card can show a little under 1:3). Closed after 3 trading days if neither is hit. No cancel at 50%.

Session is shown as information only: the backtest didn't need it. The CRT session and direction rules don't apply to this strategy.
