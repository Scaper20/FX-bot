Performance

How to read this. Each trade is compared with what chance gives for its own stop and target distances. On a coin-flip market the average result is minus the spread, which is the "chance avg R". Edge = your average minus that. z says how unusual the edge is: under 2 can easily be luck, and you need about 50 or more finished trades before it means much. The question these tables answer: do your Takes do better than your Skips, and better than chance?
