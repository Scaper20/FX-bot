# Toni Iyke running notes (raw, per video)

## T11 Jan 2026 Market Structure (part 1, 0-58m)
- Strategy = 4 concepts: market structure, liquidity, POI, directional bias. Explicitly does NOT use FVGs ("not part of the strategy"). Teaches 2 POIs (OB + breaker), liquidity types incl. how to TP with liquidity, then directional bias.
- Market structure = current flow; 3 patterns: uptrend (HH/HL), downtrend (LH/LL), range (horizontal S/R).
- Two expectations: reversal vs continuation. Reversal only in trending markets; continuation in trends and in ranges (break of range then trade in breakout direction / break & retest).
- "We trade continuations more times than reversals." Reversal happens at start of new trend/end of current trend; afterwards all entries are continuations.
- Directional bias preview: daily trend sets overall buys/sells; impulse vs correction legs. Reversals expected at HTF POIs (daily OB or daily breaker; mostly breakers: "when price breaks a zone it's likely to retest it first"). A broken high is retested before continuation.
- Reversal tool = MSS (market structure shift, a.k.a. CHoCH). Def: indication current trend ending, new one beginning opposite.
  - Valid only at a pre-marked zone: (1) HTF (daily) OB/breaker, or (2) a POI with an extremely large candle body (needs extra confirmation; lets you tighten SL). "80% of the time it's at a daily POI." Random-zone MSS likely fails.
  - MSS trigger (bullish): in downtrend, price taps the zone, then instead of a LH/LL it takes out the LAST lower high and CLOSES ABOVE IT WITH A BODY candle. Bearish mirror: take out last higher low, body close below.
  - At the zone two scenarios: zone fails (trend continues) or MSS. Don't buy a zone without the MSS (BTC example: people who bought OB without MSS got stopped).
  - Skeptical of MSS at all-time highs (no HTF zone to react from).
  - Confirmation (MSS) only needed for first-time reversals, not continuations.
- Continuation tool = BOS: price keeps breaking key levels (highs/lows) while maintaining trend; after each BOS a retracement = entry opportunity.
  - He does NOT trade every single BOS (retracement location unpredictable). Trades only MULTIPLE / DOUBLE BOS: price takes out >=2 key levels (highs in uptrend / lows in downtrend) in one leg; those retracements pull into real POIs.

## T11 part 2 (58m-end)
- Multiple/double BOS: one candle leg takes out >=2 highs (uptrend) or lows (downtrend), often internal + external + an old S/R level, CLOSING beyond them with a body. Single-level BOS retracements are shallow/unpredictable -> ignore. Multiple BOS retracements pull into real POIs (often breaker).
- Structure tells buy vs sell: MSS => reversal direction; multiple BOS => continuation direction. Use ONE structure type per setup (MSS or multiple BOS, not both).
- Liquidity: many types but for ENTRIES he uses only INDUCEMENT.
  - Inducement = first valid pullback after an MSS or multiple BOS.
  - Pullback types: full retracement (opposite candle closes, then trend candle) or single-candle wick retracement (just a wick).
  - VALID only when, after the pullback, price takes out and CLOSES BEYOND the BODY CLOSE of the MSS / multiple-BOS candle (body, not wick). Until then, keep waiting; later pullbacks can become the inducement.
  - Inducement = extra confluence that the MSS/BOS is valid; don't enter at it. Price must come back and take out the inducement, then go to the POI CLOSEST to the inducement -> entry.
  - Volatile market -> single-candle inducement; average speed -> multi-candle.
  - "Few scenarios" where price hits POI without inducement; rule: no inducement -> no trade.
- POIs: (1) Order block; (2) "failed zones" = breaker / mitigation / rejection blocks generalized: any zone price broke through; body of broken zone becomes POI. Rules for choosing between them taught in next class.
- Order block def: bullish OB = bearish candle followed by bullish candle that engulfs it (mirror for bearish).
  - Entry: if OB wick on entry side is shorter than body -> entry at the wick end; if wick longer than body -> entry at body. SL beyond the whole OB incl. wicks on SL side (he personally sometimes tighter; beginners cover wicks).
- Full model: structure (MSS or multi-BOS) -> inducement (validated by body-close break) -> price takes inducement -> POI closest to inducement (OB/failed zone) -> entry, SL beyond POI.
- TF flexibility: 1H vs 30m POIs align; scale TF up/down if POI unclear.
- R:R: smallest ~1:5; often 1:9-1:13 targeting next highs. Limit vs market entry: personal choice.

## T06 Jan 2026 Part 2 (POIs + 4 POI rules)
- POIs: OB and "breaker block" = failed zones (generalised breaker/mitigation/rejection). Breaker = BODY of each high/low that price broke (and closed beyond) while creating the MSS or multiple BOS. OB = engulfing pair that triggered the move.
- Only look for POIs after MSS / multiple BOS candle has CLOSED. Never mark breakers from single BOS.
- MSS: taking out 1 level is enough (reversal at a pre-marked zone). Multiple BOS (continuation) needs >1 level; refined Jan 2026: needs an INTERNAL zone (high/low made in current trend) + an EXTERNAL zone (high/low made during the previous opposite leg) taken out. Two internal highs alone often fail (no retrace to breaker). Not required to be one candle (usually is). Simplified version: just >1 level.
- Marking: each broken level (body) = breaker #1, #2 ...; plus OB. Breakers/OB on the same line = one POI.
- 4 RULES of picking POI:
  1. Must have MSS or multiple BOS (structure).
  2. Must have inducement (first pullback after it that then takes out body close of MSS/BOS candle).
  3. POI must be UNMITIGATED: if the inducement leg touched the BODY of a candidate POI (wick-only is ok), that POI is disqualified. Mitigation only counts after the MSS/BOS (touches before MSS don't count).
  4. Of remaining POIs, pick the one CLOSEST to the inducement (trace down from inducement; first POI you meet).
- Breaker SL: cover entire candles of breaker; if too long, put SL near the next (deeper) POI that shouldn't be reached.
- Example R:Rs 1:7, 1:11, 1:9. Entries/analysis on the SAME TF; he mostly trades 1H (day trader), goes lower if very volatile. "Time frame does not matter."
- Don't stack positions.

## T22 Mar 2026 Liquidity / Take profit (0-47m)
- Daily routine: bias -> structure (continuation vs reversal) -> 4 POI rules -> entry. "We enter with liquidity and exit with liquidity."
- TREND LINE LIQUIDITY (most common): any trend accumulates liquidity on the OPPOSITE side (uptrend -> below the higher lows; downtrend -> above the lower highs). Draw the trend line only through swings that connect SEAMLESSLY (don't force; exclude swings after a big drop that breaks the line). Liquidity extends to where that trend ORIGINATED.
- TP for REVERSAL trades: target = origin of the previous trend (full trend line liquidity sweep). May take days; can take partial TP at nearer lows and rejoin via continuation entries (multiple BOS -> inducement -> POI) still targeting the trend-line origin.
- CRITICAL MISTAKE: don't trade against the draw. Until the trend-line liquidity is cleared, any POI INSIDE that liquidity zone (OBs/breakers in the old trend's area) is OFF LIMITS for counter trades. "Don't trade POIs inside a liquidity pool."
- TP for CONTINUATION trades: if the reversal's trend-line TP is still untaken -> use it. If already taken -> watch the pattern price makes WHILE APPROACHING your POI; if it approaches in a trend (e.g. HH/HL into a sell POI), that approach trend creates trend-line liquidity -> TP at where that approach trend originated.
- First-time reversals are tricky (may miss inducement/POI); if missed, join with continuation entries.
- (cont.) Wedges: falling wedge (LH/LL approach) -> TP where it started (top); rising wedge -> TP at its origin (bottom). Same idea as approach-trend liquidity.
- RANGING LIQUIDITY: range accumulates BOTH buy-side (above) and sell-side (below). Before breaking out, price first takes the side where YOUR POI is (triggers POI), then goes to the opposite side of the range = TP. Ranges mostly on slow pairs / after impulsive moves / low liquidity (example NZDCAD, ~2-month range). Don't trade POIs INSIDE the range against the move; only POIs outside the range. Take TP at the far side of range (holding beyond got stopped in his example).
- SWING POINT LIQUIDITY (his name for external/internal range liquidity): last swing high (for buys) / last swing low (for sells) formed BEFORE price triggered your POI = nearest draw on liquidity; "works every time if trading the right direction"; highest win-rate TP. Price often exceeds it, but go there first.
- TP chosen by liquidity location, NOT by R:R target ("I don't care about RR; my focus is where liquidity is"). Will take 1:4-1:5 if that's where liquidity is.
- Note: he mostly trades commodities & crypto (gold, BTC), more volatile -> fewer ranges.

## T12 Oct 2025 Directional Bias (0-49m)
- Bias TFs: DAILY or 4H only (mostly daily). Get bias ONCE and trade it for days/weeks (e.g. gold sells 16-30 Oct) until the bias target is hit. Don't re-analyze bias every candle; don't take setups against it.
- Two ways to get bias: (A) trend method: read daily/4H trend (HH/HL vs LH/LL vs range); (B) apply the SAME MSS/multiple-BOS + inducement + POI model on daily/4H; the HTF target = bias until hit.
- Trend method steps:
  1. Identify daily/4H trend (shrink chart).
  2. Mark where the next higher low (uptrend) / lower high (downtrend) should form: HTF (daily/4H) breaker blocks or OBs, ideally confluent with a seamlessly drawn trend line touch ("double confluence"). Not every pair respects trend lines; HTF POI alone is fine.
  3. Wait for price to reach that HTF zone (doesn't have to be perfect, inside the zone).
  4. CONFIRM on a lower TF with a REVERSAL concept: MSS -> inducement -> POI tap. Daily zone -> confirm on 4H (his preference) or 2H; 4H zone -> confirm on 1H/2H; also 30m/45m ok. Confirmation is a ONE-TIME thing per new leg (not needed for later continuation entries).
  5. Once confirmed, bias = trend direction; target = the last HTF high (uptrend) / last HTF low (downtrend) -> bullish until that high is taken.
- After confirmation you can trade any LTF entries (continuations) in bias direction.
- "Price always retests daily zones" -> that retest is where the confirmation forms.
- Logic rule: if uptrend confirmed a HL, the next logical move is a HH; don't sell OBs before the HH is made.
- (T12 cont.) Confirmation may be imperfect on 4H (thick candles: spike into zone then reclaim still counts). Once confirmed, trade any TF (he uses 1H) with CONTINUATION = double/multiple BOS -> inducement -> POI rules; TP = nearest draw (swing/trend line liq.) as long as it doesn't exceed overall bias target. Example: one daily confirmation (16 May) -> buys for ~3.5 months until target hit (Sep).
- If you missed the confirmation live but can see it happened at the zone, you can still trade the new direction.
- Method B on HTF: daily MSS/DBOS -> inducement -> nearest POI tapped -> bias in that direction; target = last high/low before POI (nearest draw) / trend line liquidity.
- All-time highs: price tends to keep going (experience), but he's skeptical of reversals there.
- News days / broker differences: zone may tap on MT5 but not TradingView.
- Q&A rules:
  - Inducement validity: the break of the MSS/DBOS candle close must be by a BODY close; wick-only = not valid.
  - First-time reversal: if an MSS's POI hasn't been tapped, any DBOS formed afterwards is NOT valid; wait for MSS POI tap. Once reversal confirmed, use DBOS for continuations (MSS for reversals, DBOS for continuations).
  - If POI never triggers: don't enter; move on (if the new leg completes a new HH, forget it).
  - Trend-line liquidity "POIs inside liquidity" restriction only applies once market has started reversing against that trend; while trend continues, POIs inside the trend are fine.
  - Doesn't use premium/discount ("anything I didn't teach, I don't trade").
  - DBOS candle closing only slightly beyond a level still counts.

## T02 Taking profit with liquidity (older, 2025 version)
- Liquidity types listed: trend line (uptrend/downtrend), EQUAL HIGHS/LOWS (double top/bottom = liquidity, not a reversal signal), LONG-WICK liquidity (news spikes; price returns to clear the wick), INDUCEMENT.
- "If liquidity hasn't been taken from one end, don't expect a reversal." After structure shifts, price clears the full trend-line liquidity, then triggers the POI closest to that trend line -> only then look for reversal.
- TP options: clear approach trend-line liquidity; equal highs/lows formed before your POI; long wick before your POI; the opposite inducement; or a VALID opposing POI (one that caused a shift, has liquidity, unmitigated) as TP.
- Two breakers: pick the one closest to liquidity (inducement).
