## Part B — Gap topics

### B1. How to select a valid CRT candle (fills the gaps on "key level selection" and "which candle")
Main source: Sham, [How to Select/Find CRT Correctly?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ue9K2crfFEw). It agrees with ABFreeFX, [How to Select the RIGHT Candle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wkZpIw1isp4), and Smart Risk, [CRT 2.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PLqPSoasWK0).

**First decide what the CRT is for:**
- **CRT for bias:** Daily or Weekly candles. They set the direction for the next few days.
- **CRT for framing trades:** 4H candles. They are used for execution, with confirmation on 15m.

**Three-step filter. All three must pass, otherwise reject the candle:**
1. **Structure and context**
   - Structure gives direction: only take bullish CRTs in bullish structure and bearish CRTs in bearish structure.
   - Context gives location. Is the candle forming at a meaningful **key level**? Is it in **discount (for buys) / premium (for sells)** of the active dealing range? Has the range already been rebalanced?
   - **Dealing range** = the most recent expansion swing. If price still needs to retrace into the premium (or discount) half of that range, the short-term draw is the retracement. Don't sell the first bearish candle inside a bearish trend until the dealing range has been rebalanced.
   - Best key levels (ABFreeFX): **old highs and old lows**, then FVGs/imbalances. The candle that sweeps the CRT high or low should ideally **also sweep the key level**, even when the CRT candle itself sits just short of it.
   - Other locations that work (Smart Risk): supply/demand zones, the end of a pullback at a Fib retracement, and major structure levels that have been tested several times.
2. **Timing**
   - For bias CRTs (daily), check the weekly profile, the weekly OHLC (has the week already expanded?) and the monthly OHLC.
     - A **Monday daily candle as the range** works well: Tuesday or Wednesday sweeps it and makes the low or high of the week, then expansion follows.
     - A Thursday or Friday candle as the range is ambiguous and lower quality.
   - For 4H CRTs:
     - Check the **daily profile**: is today making its high or low first?
     - Check **key times**: London open kill zone, NY open, and "macros" such as **03:00 NY**.
     - Watch the **last hour of the 4H candle**, because how it closes changes how you read the setup.
   - The **purge (sweep) time matters more than the formation time** (ABFreeFX). The sweep should happen at a key time.
3. **Confirmation**
   - Bias CRT: the **sweeping candle must close back inside** the range. A sweep without that close-back is just a liquidity grab, so don't use it.
   - 4H CRT: after the sweep (at a key time), drop to **15m** and wait for a **CISD / structure shift** or an order block. You don't need to wait for the 4H candle to close.
   - Smart Risk: if C2 **closes beyond** the range, the CRT is invalid (expect continuation instead). For pullback entries, apply this test candle by candle until one sweeps and closes back inside.
   - When two candles sit inside a bigger candle, use the **bigger outer candle** as the range (Sham's example of an inside-bar cluster).

### B2. Time model for 4H CRTs: 1 / 5 / 9 AM New York
Source: Trade Hub, [CRT Time Algorithm (1,5,9)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q85aHMrSyo4). Images: gap_topics/01–03.
- 4H candles in NY time open at 01:00, 05:00 and 09:00.
  - **01:00 candle = range (C1)**
  - **05:00 candle = manipulation (C2, turtle soup)**
  - **09:00 candle = distribution (C3)**
- Rules:
  - The 05:00 candle **must sweep the 01:00 range and close** before you act.
  - Then, in the **09:00–10:30 NY window**, enter on the order block or FVG that caused the 5-min MSS.
  - Stop: just beyond the OB. Target: the opposite side of the range or the previous swing.
  - If the sweep or MSS happens **outside** 05:00–09:00 (e.g. at 10:00), skip it.
- Set the chart time zone to New York (UTC−4 in summer / UTC−5 in winter).
- This matches Romeo's "the 4H range is one of my favourites" and "time > price". The specific 1/5/9 schedule is this creator's, not something Romeo confirmed publicly.

### B3. Weekly profiles: which day makes the high or low of the week
Source: BandzTrading, [ICT Weekly Profiles Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kIYLbuIkEAk). This is a small channel summarising ICT's free core content. Images: gap_topics/04–10. It fits Romeo's "Mon–Wed one extreme, Thu–Fri the other".

Start from the HTF bias, a **discount array** (FVG or old low) below price and a **draw** (old high) above it. The day the price reaches the array usually makes the low of the week (bullish case; mirror image for bearish).

| Profile | Pattern | Bot action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Classic Tuesday low | Monday hovers above the array. Tuesday drops into it (low of the week). Wed–Fri expand. | Look for longs at the array on Tuesday. |
| 2. Wednesday low | Mon–Tue drift lower but miss the array. Wednesday tags it. | If Tuesday missed it, watch Wednesday. |
| 3. Wednesday weekly reversal | After a long decline, Mon–Tue consolidate above an old low. Wednesday sweeps it and reverses hard. | The sweep plus a close back above is the tell. |
| 4. Consolidation Thursday reversal | Mon–Wed tight range with the array untouched. Thursday (often on news) runs under the range into the array and reverses. | Don't give up on the week by Wednesday. |
| 5. Consolidation midweek rally | Already bounced off the array. A Mon–Wed pause, then a break of the pause high toward the draw. | No deep drop needed. |
| 6. Seek & destroy | Mon–Thu raids both sides (often before big news). Only Friday picks a side. | **Sit out.** No trade. |

Rules:
- Never call the low on Monday if the array hasn't been reached.
- Once the price breaks out of the Mon–Wed range in the bias direction, stop hunting for the low.
- No bullish profile puts the low on Friday.
- Entry: on the expected day, use 5-min (sweep → CISD → FVG), often around 09:30 NY.

### B4. Candle closing logic and "two-candle CRT"
- Both Sham and Romeo stress how a candle **closes**: close-back-inside confirms a range, and a close beyond it invalidates the range (continuation).
- If C2 already expands strongly (no C3 entry), Romeo calls that a two-candle CRT. Log it as a miss rather than chasing.

  context_ok   : sweep hits key level (old high/low first, then FVG) AND
                 price in discount (buys) / premium (sells) of active dealing range
  timing_ok    : daily CRT -> range candle Mon (or Tue), sweep Tue/Wed
                 4H CRT    -> range = 01:00 NY candle, sweep in 05:00 candle,

                 weekly    -> skip "seek & destroy" weeks (both sides raided Mon–Thu)

                 4H           -> 15m/5m CISD or MSS after sweep
invalidate if C2 CLOSES beyond the range
